MG Pilot

MG Pilot

Driven by Safety

MG sets the standard for safety with the MG Pilot Safety Suite by ensuring a safe, relaxing, and comfortable driving experience. The systems that comprise MG Pilot are:

  • Traffic Jam Assist
  • Intelligent Speed Limit Assist
  • Intelligent High Beam Assist
  • Adaptive Cruise Control
  • Driver Attention Alert
  • Blind Spot Detection
  • Lane Change Assist
  • Rear Cross Traffic Alert
  • Door Opening Warning
  • Lane Keep Assist with Lane Departure Warning System
  • Active Emergency Braking with Pedestrian and Bicycle Detection
